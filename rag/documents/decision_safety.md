# Decision safety

This application provides decision support only. It does not operate pumps, valves, machinery or spraying equipment. A farmer or qualified field adviser must review every action before use.

When field data is missing, uncertain or conflicting, lower confidence and request a field check rather than inventing a value. Keep the entered measurements and the reasoning visible in the report.

Image screening is preliminary. Lighting, camera angle, crop variety and growth stage can change what a photo shows. The prototype is not a validated disease or pest diagnostic tool.

Weather shown in the MVP is manually entered demo data. It is not a live forecast. Check a trusted local weather source before time-sensitive work.
