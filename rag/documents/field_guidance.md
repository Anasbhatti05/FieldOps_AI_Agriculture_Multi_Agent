# Field check guidance

DEMO GUIDANCE - Review with a local agriculture extension officer before using in a real field. Take soil readings at more than one representative spot and near the active root area. Record the crop and growth stage with each reading.

بارش کی پیش گوئی دیکھ کر پانی کا فیصلہ اکیلے نہ کریں۔ کھیت کی مٹی اور فصل کی جڑوں کے پاس نمی بھی چیک کریں۔

Forecast rain can change irrigation timing. Confirm the local forecast and recheck soil after rain; a forecast is not proof that rain reached the field.

An irrigation volume estimate depends on field area, soil texture, root depth, crop stage, current moisture, irrigation efficiency and local water conditions. This prototype uses uncalibrated demonstration assumptions; do not treat its number as a prescription.

کسی ایک پتے یا تصویر سے بیماری یا کیڑے کی پکی شناخت نہیں ہوتی۔ متاثرہ اور صحت مند پودوں کا موازنہ کریں اور زرعی ماہر سے مشورہ لیں۔

Do not apply pesticide from an unconfirmed image screening. Confirm the problem with a qualified local adviser and follow the registered product label and local rules.
